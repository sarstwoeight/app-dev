# ♠️ *Alice in Borderland*

## 📖 Overview  
**Alice in Borderland** is a Japanese sci-fi thriller series based on the manga by *Haro Aso*.  
It follows **Arisu**, a gamer who, along with his friends, gets trapped in a deserted version of Tokyo where they must play deadly games to survive.  

---

## ⭐ Why I Like It  
- Intense and unpredictable **survival games** 🎮  
- Deep **psychological and moral themes**  
- Amazing **cinematography** and **visual effects**  
- Characters with real emotional growth  

---

## 🧠 Main Characters  
- **Arisu (Ryohei Arisu)** — Smart, observant, and strategic  
- **Usagi (Yuzuha Usagi)** — Brave and athletic survivor  
- **Chishiya (Shuntaro Chishiya)** — Calm and calculating genius  

---

## 💬 Memorable Quote  
> “In this world, you win or you die. There’s no in-between.”
